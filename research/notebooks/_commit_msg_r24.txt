chore(rules): add R24 — process discipline for limited-context models

Codifies five patterns that became increasingly important during the
02e session under Composer 2: halt-and-surface gates between phases,
structural-finding-first synthesis, pre-emptive surfacing of verification
changes, run-state checks per turn, and the single-agent invariant.

These were applied ad-hoc during 02e via per-turn user reminders;
codifying them removes the need for per-turn reinforcement and lets
the model read them fresh on every turn.
