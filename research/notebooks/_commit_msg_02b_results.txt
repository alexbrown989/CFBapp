Findings worth surfacing in git log:

(1) opening_drive_was_explosive_td passes 3/3 Brier-improving. This
    is the empirical instantiation of the core project hypothesis --
    a single explosive scoring play on the underdog's opening drive
    carries signal about subsequent comeback equity, distinct from
    a generic dog-scored or generic-TD effect.

(2) fav_def_epa_first_drive is the only feature across 02a + 02b to
    pass 3/3 on BOTH Brier AND ECE. Structurally important for N03:
    Kelly stake sizing reads directly off calibrated probabilities,
    so a feature that improves ranking AND calibration is rarer and
    more valuable than one that improves ranking alone.

(3) Drive-volume features all FAIL stability:
      opening_drive_yards   1/3 Brier-improving
      opening_drive_plays   0/3 Brier-improving
      opening_drive_seconds 0/3 Brier-improving
    Meaningful negative result: once the binary outcome of the
    opening drive is known (dog_scored, TD, explosive TD), the
    drive's *shape* (yards / play count / clock) carries no
    independent predictive value. N03 can drop drive-shape features
    from consideration entirely.
